## Anti-Patterns Verdict

- **LLM Assessment**: The dashboard has successfully eliminated all remnants of generic purple-indigo gradients and text gradient styles, replacing them with a bespoke, theme-appropriate slate and amber interface.
- **Deterministic Scan**: Zero warnings. The app is clean and styling is consistent.

## Overall Impression
The visual coherence of the dashboard has improved significantly. By removing the AI-template gradients and pure-black backdrops, and adding clean interactive tooltips, the interface feels like a professional-grade visual observation utility.

## What's Working
- **Cohesive Brand Accent**: The Amber theme highlights feel integrated, highlighting active state elements naturally.
- **High-contrast Typography**: The headings and metrics use high-contrast solid text colors, maximizing night-vision readability.

## Persona Verification

- **Alex (Power/Dark-Sky Observer)**: Night vision is preserved. Bright violet text is gone, and the new translucent themed map layer switcher overlays fit the screen seamlessly.
- **Jordan (First-Time Stargazer)**: Hovering over table column headers now provides immediate definitions for Elevation, Azimuth, Magnitude, and Visibility, explaining what they mean and how to use them.
