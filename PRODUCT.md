# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Stargazers, hobbyist astronomers, and visual observers checking sky conditions, cloud cover, and ISS transits in real-time under dark/night environments.

## Product Purpose
A sleek, high-fidelity Weather & Astronomy Dashboard that displays key atmospheric and astronomical conditions (cloud cover, twilight ranges, celestial body positioning, moon phases, and ISS tracking coordinates) to help observers plan stargazing sessions.

## Brand Personality
Precise, immersive, utilities-first. 3-word personality: Instrument, Midnight, Amber.

## Anti-references
- Bright, neon-saturated standard weather apps that ruin night vision.
- Generic cards containing big numbers and tiny labels with rainbow gradient backgrounds.
- Em dashes, emojis, and side-stripe card borders.

## Design Principles
- **Maintain Night Vision**: Use dark slate neutrals with low-chroma amber highlights to prevent screen glare.
- **Instrument-Grade Detail**: Display dense but clean data tables and coordinate plots instead of vague summaries.
- **Frictionless Navigation**: Ensure easy keyboard navigation, high contrast text, and clear tap targets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast ratio compliance (at least 4.5:1 contrast for regular text).
- Visible focus rings and clear labels for SRE-style glancing.
