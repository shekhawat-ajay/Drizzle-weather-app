# Drizzle Credits

## Data APIs

- [Open-Meteo Forecast](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) — current, minutely-15, hourly, and 14-day daily weather data
- [Open-Meteo Air Quality](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/air-quality-api) — hourly pollutants (PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, SO₂, NH₃, CO, O₃) and EU/US AQI
- [Open-Meteo Geocoding](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api) — city search and coordinates
- [wheretheiss.at](https://wheretheiss.at/) — live ISS position, velocity, and orbital trajectory
- [CelesTrak](https://celestrak.org/) — ISS two-line elements (TLE) used for local 7-day pass predictions

- [Recharts](https://recharts.github.io/) — charts
- India NAQI computed per the [CPCB National Air Quality Index](https://cpcb.nic.in/displaypdf.php?id=bmF0aW9uYWwtYWlyLXF1YWxpdHktaW5kZXgvRmluYWwtTkFRUUktQm9va2xldC5wZGY=) breakpoint tables
